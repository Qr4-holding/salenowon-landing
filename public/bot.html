SaleNowOnBot

SaleNowOnBot is the crawler for Sale Now On (salenowon.co.uk). It reads retailers' public sale pages to list current offers, which link shoppers back to the retailer's own site.

How it behaves

It identifies itself as SaleNowOnBot/0.1 (+https://salenowon.co.uk/bot), follows each site's robots.txt, reads only public sale and category pages, and makes a few requests a day per site, slowly.

Opting out

To stop it, add this to your robots.txt and it will stop on its next visit:

User-agent: SaleNowOnBot
Disallow: /

To have your offers removed or corrected, or to be featured, email hello@salenowon.co.uk.
